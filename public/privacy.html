Privacy Policy for Resko

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Resko is an app for managing a residential society — sharing expenses, generating water and maintenance bills, tracking payments, and posting notices. It also includes a personal expense tracker that works on its own, without joining any society.

This policy explains exactly what the app stores, why, and who can see it.

Who is responsible

Resko is operated by an individual developer. For any question about this policy or your data, contact vramchandu27@gmail.com.

Your personal expense tracker is private to you. Nobody else can read it — not your society's admin, not other residents. It is stored separately from all society data and is never included in any shared view or report.

What we do not collect

  • No location or GPS data.
  • No contacts, call logs or SMS messages.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • No card numbers, bank details or payment credentials. Resko never processes a payment. Payment happens outside the app through your own UPI app; Resko only records that a screenshot was submitted and that the admin confirmed it.

Who can see your data

  • Your society's admin sees your name, flat number, phone number, bills, dues and anything you post or upload to the society.
  • Other residents of your society see notices and shared expense records. They do not see your individual dues or payment screenshots.
  • Other societies see nothing. Each society's data is isolated by enforced server-side access rules; membership in one gives no access to any other.
  • We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or transfer it to any third party other than the infrastructure providers below.

Service providers

Resko runs on Google Firebase, which stores and processes data on our behalf: Firebase Authentication (phone and password sign-in), Cloud Firestore (app data), Firebase Storage (photos), and Firebase Cloud Messaging (notifications). Google Play services is used to verify that the app is genuine before an SMS code is sent. Their handling of data is governed by Firebase's privacy documentation and Google’s Privacy Policy.

The app also loads its typefaces from Google Fonts the first time it runs. That request reaches Google’s font servers and, like any web request, carries your device’s IP address. No account information is sent with it.

When Resko opens another app

Some actions pass information to an app you already have installed. Sharing a bill or a notice opens WhatsApp with the message pre-filled; paying opens your UPI app with the amount and the society’s UPI id; sending a reminder opens your SMS app. In each case you see the message and choose whether to send it, and what happens next is governed by that app’s own privacy policy, not this one.

Where data is stored, and for how long

Data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure and may be processed outside India. Society records are kept for as long as the society uses Resko, because they are shared accounting history. Your personal expense entries are kept until you delete them or ask for your account to be deleted.

Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit. Access is enforced by server-side security rules rather than only in the app, so a modified copy of the app cannot read another society's data. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes by Firebase Authentication.

Your rights

  • See your data — most of it is visible in the app; email us for the rest.
  • Correct it — edit your name and photo in the app; ask your admin to correct flat details.
  • Delete it — email vramchandu27@gmail.com and we will delete your account, your profile, your uploads and your personal expense records. Shared society accounting records that others rely on may be retained with your name removed.

Children

Resko is intended for adults responsible for a household. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date above is updated and the change is announced in the app.
